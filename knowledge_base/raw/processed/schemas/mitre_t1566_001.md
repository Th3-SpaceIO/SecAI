# MITRE ATT&CK Technique: T1566.001 (Phishing: Spearphishing Attachment)

## Overview
Adversaries may send spearphishing messages with a malicious attachment in an attempt to gain access to victim systems. 

## Mitigation
- **Antivirus/Antimalware:** Use signatures or heuristics to detect malicious attachments.
- **Network Segmentation:** Limit the impact of a compromised workstation.
- **User Training:** Educate users to identify suspicious emails.

## Detection
Monitor for unusual process execution chains (e.g., Outlook spawning PowerShell) and suspicious network connections from common office productivity applications.
